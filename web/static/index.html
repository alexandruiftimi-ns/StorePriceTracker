📍 Your location

Used to find the stores near you. The default is central Bucharest. Press Use my current location (your browser asks for permission), or paste coordinates from Google Maps (right-click a place → click the numbers to copy them).

Kaufland prices come from the nearest Kaufland within this radius.

🏪 Lidl stores you shop at

Prices and promotions can differ between Lidl stores. Pick the ones you go to, and results show each store's own prices and which store a promotion applies to. With none picked, Lidl's national price list is used.

📨 Telegram notifications optional · free

Get your shopping results on your phone. You create your own Telegram bot, which is free and takes about 2 minutes. The token and chat ID are only stored on this computer.

  1. Create your bot. In Telegram, open @BotFather, send /newbot, then choose a name (anything) and a username ending in bot (for example my_prices_bot). BotFather replies with a token that looks like 123456789:AAH…. Copy it.
  2. Paste the token here and press Check token.
  3. Message your bot. Open your new bot in Telegram , press Start or send it any message, like hi.
  4. Get your chat ID. Press Find my chat ID. It fills the box for you.
  5. Test it. Press Send test message and check Telegram.
  6. Turn it on and save:

Keep the token private: anyone who has it can control your bot. If it leaks, send /revoke to @BotFather and paste the new token here.
